Today three pages draw filters three ways: History draws two sliders, Plan and the Recipes book draw a thin funnel with a round count badge, and the live app has both a sliders sign (filter) and a funnel (empty-filter). Groceries has no Filter button. This page shows 12 icons for one standard button, all drawn the same way as the app's everyday icons: 24 grid, 2 px lines, round ends, no fill.

The button itself is the same in every option: the kit's soft square (44 px tall, 52 on the Kitchen iPad, corner 8), the icon at 20 px, then the word Filter. When filters are on it turns sage with a thin outline and reads Filter, a thin upright line, then how many (Filter2, never a round badge). It sits at the right end of the chip row, never in the tan bar.

My pick: 3, Rounded Funnel (Lucide funnel). You already accepted it on the Recipes book (11-25), Plan and Recipes already show a funnel, and it is the sign most people know for "filter". Sliders read as "settings". The History mockup uses 3 until you choose.

Tell me a number. That icon becomes the standard (STANDARDS.md and the icon register), and every page with filters switches to it.
