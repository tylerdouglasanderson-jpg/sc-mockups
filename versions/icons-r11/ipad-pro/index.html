SupperCraft icons

Every menu icon redrawn in the Groceries bag family: warm kraft, celery, onion, carrot and cream, one brown outline. Each icon has four cuts: two for the side menu (colour and plain) and two bigger, detailed ones for pages.

Pick by letter in section 3. Everything below uses my picks (Today A, Kitchen A). Dropped after review: Today C, Kitchen C, Kitchen D, Prep B and Cook B (none read well at menu size).

1. The side menu

My proposed order, at real size (20 px icons in 44 px rows). Groceries is the open page. Plain icons take the text colour, so they work in light and dark. Colour icons in dark mode keep their dark outline, so light shapes never get a chalky edge; only the dark bits that sit on the bare menu (the History arrow, the knife and pan handles, the bell's clapper, legs and lines) turn brighter.
